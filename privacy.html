Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how SiliconSoul ("we") handles data in SiliconSoul Publisher, our internal tool that publishes SiliconSoul videos to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, and reads statistics from those accounts.

Who uses the tool

Only the SiliconSoul team uses SiliconSoul Publisher, and only with SiliconSoul's own accounts. We do not offer it to the public and we do not collect data from viewers or other users.

What data we access

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to our channel, and read-only access to our channel's statistics and YouTube Analytics (views, viewer countries, subscriber changes).
  • TikTok: our account's basic profile and statistics (follower count, video view counts) and permission to publish videos to our account.
  • Instagram (Meta): our account's basic profile and insights (followers, reel views, audience countries) and permission to publish reels to our account.

Platform statistics are aggregated numbers. They contain no personal information about individual viewers.

How we use it

Only to publish our videos and to send ourselves a daily statistics report. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted as secrets in our private GitHub repository and are only used by our automated publishing jobs. Daily total counts (followers and views per platform) are kept in that private repository so we can calculate day-to-day changes.

Google API Services

SiliconSoul Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at Google security settings.

Data deletion

To remove the tool's access, revoke it in the connected account's settings (Google security settings, TikTok "Manage app permissions", or Instagram "Apps and websites"). To have any stored data deleted, email info.siliconsoul@gmail.com. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info.siliconsoul@gmail.com.
